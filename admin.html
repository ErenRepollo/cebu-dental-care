FAQ

Common questions

Is my booking confirmed right away?

Your slot is held immediately. Once the clinic confirms, a receipt with your QR code is emailed to your Gmail.

Can I book for the same day?

Online booking opens from tomorrow. For same-day or urgent care, call the clinic.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Log in, open My bookings and press Cancel. To reschedule, cancel and book a new time.

Why do I need a Gmail account?

We email your confirmation and QR receipt there, and verifying it keeps fake bookings out.
